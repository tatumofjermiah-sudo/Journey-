# Journey — The Cycle Map

> *"It's a world that grows every time you prove you can return to control."*

Journey is a discipline tracking app built on the **3–6–9 cycle progression system**. Instead of numbers and streaks, your discipline becomes a **living isometric world** — a map that grows every time you complete a cycle.

## Core Concept

Choose a fasting duration. Complete it. That is **one cycle**.

| Level | District | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Foundation District | 12 hours |
| 2 | Control District | 24 hours |
| 3 | Endurance District | 48 hours |
| 4 | Threshold District | 72 hours |
| 5 | Apex District | 144 hours |

## 3–6–9 Progression

Within each district, your structures evolve:

| Cycles | Seal | Visual |
|--------|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Unproven | Flickering, incomplete structures |
| 3 | **Third Gate** | First stable structure — proof of repetition |
| 6 | **Sixth Path** | Structures link, paths form, district comes alive |
| 9 | **Ninth Seal** | Dominant landmark — district fully claimed |

## Identity System

Earn your title based on completed cycles:

- *"Third Gate of 12"* — 3 completed 12-hour cycles
- *"Sixth Path of 24"* — 6 completed 24-hour cycles  
- *"Ninth Seal of 48"* — 9 completed 48-hour cycles

## Philosophy

This system does not punish.
- No streak loss
- No structure destruction
- No forced behavior

If you stop, the world simply **pauses**. What you have built remains.

> *"We measure what you build, not what you fail."*
