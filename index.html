About Me

Who I Am

Jermiah Tatum. 26. Father first, everything else second. I carry depth that most people never see on the surface—intensity layered beneath stillness, fire hidden under calm.

What Drives Me

The desire to be present—for my child, for myself, for the moments that matter. I process the world through reflection, through ink, through words. Introspection isn't weakness; it's how I navigate.

My Purpose

To leave something real behind. Not just a memory, but a mark—in the way I raise my child, in the honesty of my words, in the art I carry on my skin.

My rules. My life. My consequence.

Personal Symbol

Not violence—internal conflict. The daily war between who I was, who I am, and who I'm becoming. The struggle that doesn't end but builds character.

Every day is a battle. I'm still standing.

Personal Values

01

Presence

Showing up fully—for my child, for moments, for the difficult conversations nobody wants to have. Being there matters more than being perfect.

02

Honesty

Even when it's uncomfortable. Especially with myself. I've seen what dishonesty costs—in relationships, in self-respect, in the mirror at 3 a.m.

03

Growth

Not for anyone else. For the version of myself my child will one day look up to. Growth that is uncomfortable, real, and earned.

04

Sovereignty

The right to define myself on my own terms. No label, institution, or expectation gets to write my story without my consent.

05

Love as Action

Not the easy kind. The kind that shows up tired, that forgives, that stays when leaving would be simpler. Love is a verb, and I practice it daily.
