🏙 Your City

The map above shows 5 districts. Each district is tied to one fasting order. Complete fasts → buildings grow taller. Void fasts → cracks appear.

⏱ Fasting

Pick any order and hit ▶ BEGIN FAST. The timer counts down. When it reaches zero your fast is complete and you can seal it.

◆ Cycles

A sealed cycle = one completed fast. Each one adds a floor to your building. Seal 3 for Third Gate, 6 for Sixth Path, 9 for Ninth Seal.

⚑ Cracks

Hitting ⚑ VOID or sealing before time's up records a crack. Cracks show as red fracture lines on your building and lower City Stability.

🛡 Stability

Completed cycles ÷ total fasts. 100% = perfect record. Drops with every crack. Shown top-right of the header. Keep it above 80%.

⬆ Tiers & Ranks

Each district has ranks: Initiate (1+) → Third Gate (3+) → Sixth Path (6+ = spire) → Ninth Seal (9+ = gold crown).
